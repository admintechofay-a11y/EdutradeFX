FINAL DONE FORM Solutions Providers
EduTradeFX — Complete Solutions Providers Registration & Onboarding System
Consolidated developer specification
This is the complete, combined structure for your developer. It brings together the company registration form, multiple-category selection, all 10 category-specific forms, product/service registration, dropdown options, pricing, documents, verification, company profile and provider dashboard in one organised specification.
The system will follow this principle:
ONE COMPANY → MULTIPLE CATEGORIES → SEPARATE CATEGORY FORMS → MULTIPLE PRODUCTS → ONE COMPANY PROFILE.
For example, Centroid Solutions can register once, select Categories 01, 03 and 06, complete those three technical forms, add its products, and appear in all three category directories using the same company profile.
The structure below follows your Solutions Providers document and the category arrangement we developed from it.

One provider can select multiple categories.

15. Final developer rules — must follow
One company registers only once.
The company can select one or all 10 categories.
Every selected category gets a separate technical onboarding form.
Unselected category forms remain hidden.
Each company can add unlimited products and services.
Every product gets its own product registration form.
Products can be linked to multiple categories without duplication.
Pricing must be stored separately for each product.
Common company information must be entered only once.
Allow draft saving and later editing.
Allow category and product approval independently.
Private verification documents must never be displayed publicly.
Do not publish unverified claims, certifications, integrations, ratings or performance figures.
Use searchable dropdowns for long lists such as countries, cities, currencies and time zones.
Use dependent dropdowns where the second field depends on the first.
Use multi-select controls for platforms, asset classes, languages, regions and service capabilities.
Every public profile must use approved company information.
Keep the website responsive and consistent with the EduTradeFX blue-and-white design.
The final structure
ONE COMPANY = ONE MASTER PROFILE
ONE COMPANY = MULTIPLE CATEGORIES
ONE SELECTED CATEGORY = ONE CATEGORY-SPECIFIC FORM
ONE COMPANY = MULTIPLE PRODUCTS AND SERVICES
ONE PRODUCT = ONE PRODUCT RECORD, WITH MULTIPLE CATEGORY LINKS IF REQUIRED
ONE COMPANY = ONE PROVIDER DASHBOARD
ALL PUBLIC LISTINGS = ADMIN APPROVAL BEFORE PUBLICATION
